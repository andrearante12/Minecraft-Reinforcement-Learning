simple_jump is the two-block gap. Curriculum then adds one-block, three-block, diagonal, and vertical variants without restarting the env server.

Sees · 159-d

  • 6 proprio (on-ground, yaw, pitch, Δpos)
  • 3 goal Δ to the landing
  • 5×5×6 voxel occupancy

Does · 15 actions

  • Each held 0.15s
  • sprint_jump is the one that matters
  • +10 land · −5 fall · dense Z-progress

Architecture

Most of the vector is the occupancy grid looking further forward than back. Three streams keep body, landing, and terrain from washing each other out before the actor picks sprint_jump.

Full lab notes: parkour report. Live dashboard: python malmo/rl/visualization/live_viz.py --env simple_jump --checkpoint … --mock.
